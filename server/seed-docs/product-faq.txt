Northwind Robotics Product FAQ

What is the warranty period?
All robotics units carry a 24 month warranty covering manufacturing defects.
Damage from misuse is not covered.

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 working days. Express shipping takes 2 working
days and costs an additional 40 dollars.

Can I return a unit?
Units may be returned within 30 days of delivery in their original packaging for
a full refund.

What power supply is required?
All units run on 110 to 240 volts and ship with a region-appropriate adapter.
